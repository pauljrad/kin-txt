Make a little space for reading

Put notifications aside, choose a short passage and start with a pace that feels comfortable. If you lose the thread, pause and return to the last sentence. You do not need to finish a whole chapter for the time to count.

What is kinetic reading?

Kinetic reading presents words over time rather than asking you to scan an entire page. One approach displays individual words in the same position. Other approaches use rhythm, highlighting and changes in pace. KiN-TXT combines these options with traditional pages so you can choose how to read.

Does it replace normal reading?

No. Reading preferences differ, and one-word presentation is not suitable for every text or reader. You may want a full page for a complicated argument, reference material or a passage you want to revisit. KiN-TXT also offers Page mode and adjustable playback.
